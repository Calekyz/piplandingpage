FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need trading experience to start?

No. The signals and bot are designed to be usable by beginners. That said, take time to learn the basics and always practise on a demo account first.

Which account should I open first?

If you want to trade currency pairs and metals, open the forex account. If you prefer binary options and synthetic indices with 24/7 markets, go with Deriv.

Is the AI bot a guarantee of profit?

No. No bot, signal service or strategy can guarantee profits. Trading involves substantial risk and you can lose money. The bot is a tool, not a promise.

How much do I need to start?

That depends on the broker and your own risk tolerance. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose. Check the minimum deposit on the broker's own site.

Do you provide financial advice?

No. PiptraderAI provides software, tools and educational content only. Nothing on this site is personalised financial, investment or tax advice.

Are these affiliate links?
